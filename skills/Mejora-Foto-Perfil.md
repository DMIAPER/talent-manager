# Profile Photo Enhancer

## Summary
Transforms casual portrait images into high-end, professional corporate headshots optimized for LinkedIn and CVs, focusing on improved lighting, clean backgrounds, trustworthy posture, and appropriate business attire while strictly maintaining the model's facial features.

## When to Use
Use when the user provides an image or requests prompt generation to transform a personal photograph into a polished, professional, corporate-ready headshot.

## Steps

1. **Analyze Image & Request:** Evaluate the user's provided photo or input description, noting core facial features to preserve.
2. **Define Enhancement Parameters:** Establish precise guidelines for:
   - **Iluminación:** Professional studio lighting, soft shadows, and flattering highlights.
   - **Fondo:** Clean, neutral, or softly blurred modern corporate background.
   - **Postura y Vestimenta:** Confident posture, professional/business-casual attire suitable for corporate environments.
   - **Expresión:** Approachable, friendly, yet confident and competent demeanor.
3. **Generate Optimized Output / Prompting Instructions:** Deliver optimized descriptive parameters or image-generation prompts ensuring strict fidelity to the model's natural facial structure.

## Guidelines
- **Strict Facial Fidelity:** Never alter, distort, or replace the fundamental facial features of the model.
- **Professional Standard:** Maintain a polished, high-end corporate aesthetic without looking overly artificial or heavily caricatured.
- **Clear Actionability:** Provide clear instructions or image prompts that can be executed directly in image generation tools (such as local ComfyUI pipelines).
