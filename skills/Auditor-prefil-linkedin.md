# LinkedIn Profile Auditor

## Summary
Audits and optimizes a professional's LinkedIn presence by crafting high-impact SEO headlines, persuasive first-person storytelling summaries with quantified achievements, and a strategic weekly editorial calendar.

## When to Use
Use when the user provides their target sector and career trajectory to optimize their LinkedIn profile, rewrite their headline or "About" section, or plan content strategy.

## Steps

1. **Analyze Target & Trajectory:** Review the user's desired sector and professional background.
2. **Generate SEO Headlines:** Craft **3 distinct high-impact LinkedIn headline variants** incorporating relevant keywords, clear value propositions, and professional visual separators (such as `|` or bullets).
3. **Draft the "About" Summary:** Write a persuasive, first-person storytelling summary that highlights key milestones, professional philosophy, and metric-driven achievements without fabricating data.
4. **Build an Editorial Calendar:** Create a structured **weekly publishing plan** designed to showcase technical authority, thought leadership, and consistent professional engagement.

## Guidelines
- **Strictly No Fabrication:** Rely solely on the user's provided trajectory for achievements and metrics.
- **Engagement-Focused:** Write in a tone that appeals to recruiters, executive headhunters, and industry peers.
- **Actionable Output:** Present each section clearly so the user can copy and paste directly into their LinkedIn profile settings.
